Privacy Policy — Turtle Dice

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy describes how the Turtle Dice app for Android (“the app”), published on Google Play by Sea Turtle Apps (“we”), handles information.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send data over the internet.

Your dice tray, your settings and your roll history are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage, so they are still there next time you open it. They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app, clearing its storage, or tapping “Clear” in the roll history deletes them.

Device permissions

The only permission the app asks for is vibration, for the buzz when the dice land and when the turn timer runs out. It is used only on your device and involves no personal data. The app has no internet permission.

While the app is open and “Shake to roll” is on, it reads the phone's motion sensor to notice a shake and to move the 3D dice with the phone. This needs no permission. The readings are used at once on your device and are never stored or sent anywhere. Turning “Shake to roll” off stops the app from reading the sensor.

Google Play

Google Play handles the download, installation and updates of the app and processes data under the Google Privacy Policy. Google may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash reports. We cannot identify individual users from them.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you, do not share it, and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date before that version is released.
