Privacy Policy — Dice Roller

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This policy describes how the Dice Roller app for Android (“the app”), published on Google Play by Sea Turtle Apps (“we”), handles information.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send data over the internet.

Your dice, settings and roll history exist only on your device while the app is running. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Device permissions

The app uses vibration (for haptic feedback) and audio settings (to play the roll sound). These are used only on your device and involve no personal data.

Google Play

Google Play handles the download, installation and updates of the app and processes data under the Google Privacy Policy. Google may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash reports. We cannot identify individual users from them.

Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you, do not share it, and delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date before that version is released.
